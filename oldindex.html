How to read this: Positions are drawn from a research report analyzing the 2026 primaries' education debates. Where a candidate has made a documented statement, sponsored a bill, or has a voting record on a topic, it is labeled documented record. Where none exists, the position shown is projected from the candidate's party alignment, not something the candidate has personally said. This is a research summary, not an official voter guide — confirm positions with candidates directly before voting.
